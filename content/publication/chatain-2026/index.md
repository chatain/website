abstract: >-
  Value capture is a central concept in strategic management theories. The field has developed
  multiple theories of value capture which can often be usefully reformulated and subsumed under the
  value-based framework (Brandenburger & Stuart, 1996). It provides a common language articulated around
  notions of value creation and value capture and how the former enables the latter. Its strengths are
  its conceptual generality and sophistication, and its ability to complement and play well with other
  theories in strategic management. This entry provides an account of how value capture theories matter
  to strategic management thinking, of the nuts and bolts of the value-based framework, and surveys some
  of its contributions and connections to other theories.
